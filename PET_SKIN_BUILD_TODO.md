## Sources

- Primary build source: `assets/source-cache/Reverse-1999-CN-Asset/mappings/ArcanistMap.json`.

- Package naming rule: `9Pets-Name-Skin-Name` and `9Pets-Cute-Name-Skin-Name`; original/default skins use `Default`.
- Baby Blue and Balloon Party have no usable Normal Live2D path in the asset map (`live2d/roles/` only), so all their Normal skin rows are `N/A` until a real Normal source is found; their Cute skin work remains separate.
- Non-default Normal skin rows stay pending until the matching skin Live2D source has been extracted and audited; previous portrait/elastic-rig outputs are not accepted as completed skin packages.
- Non-default Cute skin rows stay pending until an audited chibi Spine render profile exists for that exact skin package; `tools/build_pets_site.py --cute-only ... --skin ...` refuses those packages without one.
